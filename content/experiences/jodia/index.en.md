### Description de mission
I worked on a fixed-term contract as a Gameplay Programmer, using the UE5 engine, at the company **Jodi-A Editions**. I had the opportunity to work on the refactoring and QA for the game [Sliding Bears](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3047570/Sliding_Bears/) (available on Steam), as well as on a second, unannounced project.


### Sliding Bears
Sliding Bears is an arcade-style party game for everyone, in which you play as little bears that slide across ice and have to crash into each other to inflict damage. There are power-ups to spice things up.

On this project, I was tasked with rethinking and re-architecting a large part of the game’s Blueprint codebase, as well as developing various gameplay elements. My responsibilities included QA, implementation and UI design. I also helped implement the multiplayer feature via Steam.
