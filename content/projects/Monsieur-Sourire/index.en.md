## Introduction
I had the chance to take part in my second GGJ, once again using Unreal Engine 5. This time, most of the team had no prior experience with UE. This gave me the opportunity to help them learn the engine.

## Results
During this jam weekend, we managed to create a game on the theme of laughter, in which the aim is to send poo into the big bad guy’s mouth. To do this, you simply need to make him laugh on stage using various objects.

## Conclusion
It’s always a pleasure to take part in Game Jams; there’s always a challenge, and it’s a great way to break out of the routine of everyday projects!
