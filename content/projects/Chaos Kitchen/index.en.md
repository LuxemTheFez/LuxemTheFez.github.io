## Introduction
As part of a Gamagora Master's 2 course titled "Interface", we were tasked with developing a VR game proof of concept (POC), over a three-week period working on it only a few hours a week. Based on this premise, three of my friends and I decided to create a game inspired by Overcooked but with a twist, in which we had to "hunt for food". As this was a proof of concept and our first VR game on Unreal, the mechanics are very basic and the objective is simply to make a burger.

## Results
We therefore produced a proof of concept for a VR game using Unreal.

As this was a university project, the game is far from complete, but it allowed me to get back into VR development, this time using the Unreal Engine.
